# First-Use Usability Validation

## Protocol

- Recruit 10 participants who have not used this application before.
- Give each participant access to two browser sessions or a partner's invitation link.
- Ask them to create a match, choose a clock preset, join as the second player, make a
  legal move, and identify whose turn follows.
- Do not provide guidance during the attempt. Record completion and any point where the
  participant needed assistance; do not record names or other personal information.
- A participant succeeds only if they complete all listed actions without assistance.
- The criterion passes when at least 9 of 10 participants succeed.
